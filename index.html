1. Overview

Pocket Vault Pro ("the App") is an offline-first mobile game collection developed by Mayukh Mukherjee under the studio name Trismar Games. Your privacy is important to us. The App does not require user accounts, logins, or personal profile registration, and we do not operate developer servers that collect or store your personal information.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All gameplay progress—including XP, levels, coin balances, high scores, quests, unlocked gear, audio/haptic preferences, and saved game states—is stored strictly locally on your device using on-device storage. This data is never transmitted to the developer. You can delete all locally stored data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling the App.

3. Optional Advertising (Google AdMob)

When your device has an active internet connection, the App offers optional Rewarded Video Ads powered by Google AdMob (Google LLC). When you are offline, no ads are requested or loaded.

To deliver, measure, and prevent fraud in advertisements, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain technical data, including:

  • Device or other identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App interactions (such as ad impressions and clicks)
  • Crash logs and diagnostic performance data

All data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, the App requests advertising consent via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can opt out of personalized advertising or reset your Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Google → Ads.

For details on how Google uses information from apps that use its services, please review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from partner apps.

4. Children's Privacy

Pocket Vault Pro is designed for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party service in the App, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
